Install it to play full screen, keep your saves, and play offline.

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Tap Share in Safari's toolbar (at the bottom, or at the top on iPad).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Halo CE from your Home Screen. It runs full screen and keeps its data.

In Chrome or Edge on iPhone, Share is at the top right. Safari cannot add a site to the Home Screen by itself, so these steps are needed once.

Install the app

  1. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.
  3. Open Halo CE from your home screen or app drawer.

Samsung Internet: tap ☰, then Add page to, then Home screen.

Game data

The game needs the maps folder of your own Xbox disc image of Halo: Combat Evolved (.iso or .xiso, any version). It is copied into this app's private storage (up to 1.8 GB); the disc image itself can be deleted afterwards. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Add as many disc images as you like (a multiplayer disc, a full game, a modded one) and pick the one to play. Each keeps its own saved games.
